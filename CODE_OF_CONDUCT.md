# Code of conduct

Armonìa is committed to a respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous community.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- accept constructive disagreement;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and unwanted personal attention;
- respect privacy and security-reporting boundaries;
- make room for different experience levels and communication styles.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports should use the private contact channel declared by the repository owner.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and protect the reporter's privacy.
