# Code of Conduct

Armonia is built for developers with different languages, tools, backgrounds,
and levels of experience. Participation requires professional, constructive
behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not a person's worth or intent.
- Make room for questions and clearly explain hidden assumptions.
- Accept correction, credit prior work, and disclose relevant conflicts.
- Protect private repository content and security reports.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to reject a rule that is noisy, unsafe, or
  insufficiently evidenced.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure
of private information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or
ban a participant. Reports will be handled with discretion and conflicts of
interest will be delegated where possible.
